How to use this page
  1. Add fuel below: pick the vehicle, the liters, and the dates. The liters are shared equally across the days.
  2. Every Monday morning and Friday afternoon, type each vehicle's actual odometer in Weekly odometer readings. Friday afternoon is also next Monday morning (vehicles are not used on weekends), so Monday is filled in for you.
  3. Monitoring shows each day's fuel and the odometer worked out from it. Bold = a reading you typed; grey italic = computed.
  4. Checks finds mistakes, Maintenance shows which vehicles are due for PM, and Reports gives the monthly summary.

Add fuel by date range

Liters are split equally across the days (From to To, inclusive). Overlapping entries add up. Fuel requisitions marked Issued in Request monitoring are added automatically (tagged with their FR no.), so don't type those again.
