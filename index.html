Our services

Support across every stage of the equipment lifecycle.

Procurement & planning

Needs assessment, specifications, and acquisition planning aligned with budget cycles.

Inventory & tagging

Asset registration, property tagging, accountability records, and annual physical counts.

Maintenance & repair

Preventive maintenance schedules, calibration, and corrective repair with tracked work orders.

Issuance & transfer

Assignment to personnel and units, inter-office transfers, and return processing.

Disposal

Condemnation, surplus handling, and environmentally responsible disposal of unserviceable items.

Reporting

Utilization, depreciation, and condition reports to support decisions and audits.

Request maintenance or repair, or requisition fuel. We respond within one business day.
