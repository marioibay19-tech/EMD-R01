    Submit a request

    Request maintenance or repair, or requisition fuel. We respond within one business day.

    1. Submit the form with the unit's property or plate number.
    2. Review: the division checks the documents and schedules an inspection or approves the fuel.
    3. Fulfil: receive the fuel, or track your repair below until the unit is released.

      What we do

      Fleet assignment

      Assignment of heavy equipment and service vehicles to district offices and projects.

      Preventive maintenance

      Scheduled PMS by hour-meter or odometer reading to keep units serviceable.

      Repair and rehabilitation

      Pre-repair inspection, job orders, repair and post-repair inspection before release.

      Fuel, oil and lubricants

      Fuel requisition and issuance against approved trip tickets, with consumption records.

      Property records and disposal

      Accountability records, inventory, and disposal of unserviceable units.
